Auto-posting removes the repetitive copy-paste work of publishing the same
article to several blog platforms.

1. Write your article once as Markdown.
2. Configure one or more targets (WordPress, etc.).
3. Run `v2r-autopost publish` — or schedule it with `run`.

The console **dry-run** target lets you preview exactly what would be sent
before wiring up real credentials.
